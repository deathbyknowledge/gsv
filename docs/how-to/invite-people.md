# One person per Ship {#one-person-per-space}

Each Ship has one account for you, plus the accounts Ship and its helpers run as.
To connect with another person, invite them through [People](/how-to/contact-people).
They use their own Ship; a contact invitation does not create an account in yours.
An operator can host separate Ships for a [whole team](/how-to/organisations).

## Create a Ship {#create-a-space}

Open your operator's signup page or choose **Create your Ship** in Desktop.
Choose a handle, enter your email and agree to the terms. Verify your email, then
set your password. Registration needs no invite code when the operator enables
it. One verified email can create one Ship. Sign in with the same email to reopen it.

A friend's contact link opens their invitation first. After signup, continue in
your Ship to choose who handles incoming messages and accept the invitation.
If activation is interrupted, sign in with the same email and continue activation.

## Sign in

Open your Ship and enter your password. Activation derives the internal account name
from your handle. Existing Ships keep their account identity, home directory,
credentials and connections.

## Recover personal access

Choose **Forgot your password?** on your Ship's sign-in page, then **Email me a
code**. You confirm your Ship's handle and the email you signed up with, enter the
code we send to it, and come back to your Ship to set a new password. Then sign in
as usual. The page only ever says a code was sent "if this is the email for this
Ship", so it never tells anyone which address owns a Ship.

If you connected a messenger, you can ask for a code there instead on the same
page. If your Ship has no email recovery (some Ships set up by an operator don't),
use a messenger, sign in as the administrator and open **Settings → Sign-in →
Personal sign-in**, or ask whoever runs your Ship.

A reset revokes your sessions and messenger connections; sign in again and
reconnect those messengers. Your data, account identity and running work remain.
If your administrator password is still the one you chose at signup, it changes to
the new password too, so the old one stops working everywhere. An administrator
password you set separately stays as it is.

The administrator sign-in has broader permissions. Ordinary sign-in to your
account does not grant administrator access. The emailed link can reset the
administrator password instead if that is what you lost: choose **Reset the
administrator password instead** on the page where you set a new password. See the
[security model](/architecture/security-model).
