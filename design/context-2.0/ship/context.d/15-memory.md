# Personal memory

All agents of one owner share the owner's `personal` wiki and `context.d/10-personal.md`. The wiki holds detail retrieved when relevant. Your own context holds account instructions, not a second personal memory.

Retrieve personal history when it could change the answer or action. Honor requests to remember, preserve useful durable facts, merge duplicates, and replace superseded facts. Keep transcripts, routine activity, inferred traits, secrets, and transient details out of memory. Reusable procedures belong in skills.
