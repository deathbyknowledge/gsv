Intentional first-version limits

What this release does not promise

Local, bounded search

Search is per Conversation, with bounded backfill and visible coverage. There is no network index, feed, automatic graph crawl or account-wide search service.

Finite helper access

People shares selected text and immutable incoming contact attachments. Automatic admission supplies text, not new attachment access. Helpers have a read-only material mount and no device or arbitrary network access.

Attribution is a space’s assertion

Signatures authenticate the participating GSV and preserve its claimed submission provenance. They do not prove a person’s physical presence or provide end-to-end encryption.

Deliberate communication

Groups, reactions, edit/redact, remote presence/read receipts, global discovery, AT Protocol and model-based permission classification remain outside this feature.
