HOW TO READ THE MAP

MAP GUIDE

Colors put places that work closely together into groups. Shapes show the kind of job each place does. Neither tells you that one place is healthier or more important than another.

GROUP COLORS

ENTRYfinds the right GSV home
MAIN GSVchecks and coordinates work
BUILT-IN WORKhandles GSV files and projects
SHARED RULESkeeps actions consistent
WEBSITEthe screen people use
YOUR COMPUTERapps and approved computer work
OUTSIDE SERVICESAI, accounts, and message apps
BROWSER WORKapproved work inside web pages
SETUPbuilds and connects the pieces

MAP SHAPES

SHAPEshows the kind of job, never how much code it has
LIGHTSpick one to learn about a smaller part
BASEmeans this place keeps information or setup files
CHECK LABELmarks a real check of who is asking, what they may do, or whether work is too old
CIRCLESmark areas with extra checks of who is asking and what they may do

LINES

An arrow shows something moving one way. A dashed line means two places follow the same rules, so it has no arrow.

ASKSone place asks another for work
DIRECTSone place starts, stops, or guides work
SAVES WITHinformation is kept in the other place
SHARES RULESboth places promise the same behavior
SETS UPone place builds or connects the other
STORYthe path for the story you chose

HOW TO USE THIS MAP

Explore GSV as a small city.

Each place has one main job. Pick a place to learn what it does, what it remembers, and what it must never do.

DRAG / ARROWS
Turn the map
SCROLL / + / −
Zoom in or out
CLICK
Learn about a place or one of its lights
DOUBLE-CLICK
Move one place to the center
PLACES / ABOUT / STORY
Open one explanation at a time
GUIDE
Learn what the colors, shapes, and lines mean
/ OR ⌘K
Search by a name or job
ESC
Close the current extra view

Use the four map views to answer four simple questions: What is happening? Who handles it? What keeps it safe? What is remembered?
